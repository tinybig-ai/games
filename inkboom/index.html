One tap. The whole page.

Tap the field and a blot of ink bursts open. Every orb it catches bursts too, and the chain spreads across the page. Aim well and one tap wipes the lot. Pop every orb and you win the round, whatever the target says.

Build your bag

Spend between rounds on orbs that burst bigger, grow the multiplier or roll across the field. Then evolve the ones you love.

Bosses change the page

Every fourth round brings a new arena (rings, hourglasses, rooms) and a new rule that stacks on the last.

Throw the blast

With the Slingshot, drag to send your blast rolling around walls and into the corners a tap can't reach.

Lose, but not everything

Beat a boss and your build is saved. A bad round sends you back to that checkpoint, not to round one.

Free, and it's yours

Free to play, no account, and it works offline. Your progress stays on your device; the privacy policy says exactly what it keeps.
