The gun fires itself

The deck is rising and boarders are climbing onto it. Your rifle handles the shooting — so the only question that matters is where you are standing. Get knocked off the edge and everything changes: the gun goes quiet, and the rope is the only verb you have left. Throw it at a wall, swing, and get back on before the deck climbs out of reach.

Rope or gun, never both. That one rule is the whole game.

EVERY FLOOR IS A CHOICE

Clear a floor, take a perk. Heavier rounds, bullets that punch through bodies, a hook that tears through anything in its path.

THE SHAFT TURNS HOSTILE

Past a kilometre it stops being just boarders. Laser grids with one lane through them, saw walls, falling bombs, and a blade that comes for the deck itself.

SPEND WHAT YOU EARNED

Scrap buys permanent traits, eight guns that each play differently, and a crew to carry them. A bad run still moves you forward.

ONE LEADERBOARD

Every run is posted automatically. See exactly who you just passed, and who is still above you.
